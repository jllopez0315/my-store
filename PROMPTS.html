Prompts for your store

Every prompt we use in the three sessions, plus the ones you'll use every week. Click Copy, paste it into Claude Code (or Codex) with your store folder open, and replace anything in [brackets] with your own details. One prompt, one change. Say publish only when the preview looks right.

Claude reads the CLAUDE.md file in your folder every time, so it already knows your rules. If it ever gets something wrong twice, say: "Add that to CLAUDE.md so you remember it next time."

Session 1 · Connect

Read CLAUDE.md and START-HERE.html, then tell me in five lines what this store is and what you will never do without asking.A warm-up. Confirms the assistant found your rules.
Fill in the brackets in CLAUDE.md with these details: my name is [NAME], my store is [STORE NAME] at [WEB ADDRESS], I use Path [A or B] to publish.
In store/settings.js set the brand name to "[STORE NAME]", the sub word to "[LABS or blank]", the tagline to "[TAGLINE]", and the contact email to [EMAIL]. Show me the preview.
Switch the store to the [dark or light] look, the [classic, modern, or editorial] font pair, and set the brand color to #[HEX] and the accent color to #[HEX]. Show me the preview.
Run the pre-publish check on everything you changed and tell me exactly what to look at in the preview.
PublishOnly after you've looked at the preview.
